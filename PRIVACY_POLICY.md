# Privacy Policy for Bangla Dictionary
*Last Updated: June 11, 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how **Bangla Dictionary** (the "Application"), developed by **Forhad Labs**, manages and protects data.

## 1. Scope of Service & Offline-First Design
The Application is built as a **fully offline utility**. Its primary calculations, word translations, database queries, and Text-to-Speech vocal sounds are processed directly inside your mobile device.
*   **No Central Server Collection:** We do not maintain background servers, databases, or analytics engines that collect your written translation searches on our behalf.
*   **No Internet Requirement:** The core dictionary database (Room SQLite) resides locally on your Android file system.

## 2. Personal Information Collected
We believe in absolute data minimization.
*   **Searched Vocabs & History:** All search history entries, popularity indices, and bookmark logs are stored in private local tables governed by Android’s secure app storage sandbox. When you click "Clear All" in the History tab, this information is completely deleted.
*   **Simulated Ads & Config:** Interactive Ad settings and Reward points are processed locally underneath virtual configuration managers with no network payloads directed to third-party ad bidding networks.

## 3. Remote Config & Monetization Simulations
The Application contains simulated integration slots for AdMob and AppLovin. 
*   **Consent:** If we configure real live ad SDK implementations in subsequent versions, users will be requested to grant consent before collecting device advertising identifiers. Currently, all layouts render placeholders.

## 4. Permissions Requested
*   **Volume & Audio Output:** Used only to speak words through the Android Text-to-Speech Engine.
